What Ambassadors do

No single script. Your network, your voice.

Ambassadors are trusted advocates who engage with the mission their own way — from anywhere, across campuses, communities, and conversations.

Advocate & spread awareness

Share EZCollegeApp with students, families, and educators who could benefit — through conversation, social, campus events, or community networks.

Refer & earn

Every student who subscribes with your referral code earns you a direct commission. Track referrals and conversions in real time.

Bridge communities

Connect with underserved, multilingual, or international student communities — helping EZCollegeApp reach students who need it most.

Nominate new Ambassadors

Know someone who shares the mission? Nominate motivated people from any background and grow the program through trusted networks.

About the platform

What is EZCollegeApp?

An AI-powered college counseling platform built by GyriQAI, Inc. — a UGA spin-off — so every student can get strategic guidance, not only families who can afford elite consultants.
