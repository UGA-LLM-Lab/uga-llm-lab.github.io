Large Language Models Group

About the organization

AI • Healthcare • Education • Quantum • Humanities • Social Good

Hosted by the School of Computing

The Large Language Models Group is an interdisciplinary student organization exploring how LLMs are transforming research, education, industry, and society. Its activities include foundational research discussions, educational programs, workshops, interdisciplinary collaboration, and projects applying AI to healthcare, education, quantum computing, the humanities, and social good.

Advisor

Dr. Tianming Liu

Distinguished Research Professor, School of Computing, UGA Chief AI Advisor & CEO, GyriQAI Inc.

Dr. Liu is one of the world’s leading AI researchers, with expertise spanning large language models, brain-inspired AI, biomedical imaging, and computational neuroscience. He is the founder of GyriQAI Inc. and the driving force behind EZCollegeApp’s AI architecture, leading a team of six AI researchers from UGA’s School of Computing. As the organization’s advisor, he provides senior research guidance, connects students with experienced faculty and Ph.D. researchers, and helps bridge academic ideas with real AI products and startup opportunities.

Learn more about the team’s work behind EZCollegeApp

Founder

Ruidong Zhang

Founder · Ph.D. Student in Computer Science · Entered UGA in Fall 2025

Ruidong Zhang is a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Georgia whose experience spans AI research, student leadership, and entrepreneurship. He has organized LLM Club programming at UGA, coordinated interdisciplinary talks and student engagement, and helped develop EZCollegeApp through GyriQAI, Inc., a UGA spin-off startup. These experiences showed him that many students are interested in large language models but lack a consistent community where they can learn, collaborate, and turn ideas into research or real-world projects.

He is creating the Large Language Models Group to make that path more accessible to students across disciplines. As founder, he is prepared to lead the organization’s initial planning and registration, recruit and support the founding team, coordinate faculty and industry relationships, organize events and project opportunities, and build a transparent, inclusive culture in which members share responsibility and ownership.

Build the founding team

  • Founding rolesThe President and Faculty Advisor are already in place. We are seeking founding members to serve as Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer, Research Seminar Coordinator, Workshop & Events Coordinator, Communications & Outreach Lead, and AI Startup Partnerships Lead. Responsibilities can be matched to each member’s strengths and interests.
  • CommitmentThe founding team will meet biweekly. Members will help organize research seminars and AI workshops with senior professors and Ph.D. researchers, while creating opportunities for direct engagement with AI startup product teams. Each founding member should take ownership of at least one workstream and contribute consistently between meetings.
  • Team cultureWe are building a research-driven, welcoming, and action-oriented team. Members from every discipline are encouraged to contribute ideas, ask ambitious questions, and learn from one another. Decisions will be made transparently, responsibilities will be shared clearly, and credit will be given openly. We value curiosity, reliability, respectful debate, and a willingness to turn ideas into useful events, research, and real-world projects.
