02

New venture

Find students who want to build with you

If you have an idea but have not created a startup yet, are still exploring a possible company, or want to join a student team, begin with Find Teams. You may find an existing idea that matches your interests or a proposer looking for your skills.

If you do not find a suitable match, use the Founder Profile template to prepare a short public listing. Email the completed HTML to sl64343@uga.edu and copy tliu@uga.edu.

Subject
[Team Listing] Project Name - Your Name
Include
A brief introduction to yourself, the problem or idea you want to pursue, your current progress, and the skills or collaborators you hope to find.
Template
Open the Founder Profile template or download the HTML directly, replace the example content, and keep its directory metadata fields.
