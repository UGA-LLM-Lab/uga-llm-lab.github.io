Support

Start with the option that best describes your current situation. Each path gives you a short, ordered process and the correct materials to submit.

Path 1 I already have a startup Request faculty guidance or collaboration and prepare a project page for the lab website. View this path ↓ Path 2 I have an idea or want to join a team Browse current teams first, then submit a listing if you still need collaborators. View this path ↓
01

You already have a startup

Request guidance and publish your project page

Introduce the startup first. After making contact, use one of the website templates to prepare the page that will represent your project on the lab website.

  1. 1

    Send an introductory email

    Email tliu@uga.edu and copy sl64343@uga.edu. Briefly introduce yourself, your startup, its current stage, and the guidance or collaboration you are seeking.

    Subject
    [Entrepreneurship Support] Company Name - Your Name
    Start the introductory email
  2. 2

    Download a website template

    Choose one of the three templates below. You may also use them as a starting point for a more customized page.

    Template A

    Classic

    A dark opening with a short overview and three product points.

    Preview →Download HTML ↓
    Template B

    Technical

    A light layout with metrics and a three-step system summary.

    Preview →Download HTML ↓
    Template C

    Impact

    A warm layout focused on users and measurable impact.

    Preview →Download HTML ↓
  3. 3

    Customize the template

    Replace the example content with your startup's information, adjust the colors and layout when needed, and prepare any corresponding high-resolution images. Delete the template's download area before submission.

  4. 4

    Send the completed page

    Email the finished HTML file and all corresponding high-resolution images to sl64343@uga.edu.

    Subject
    [Startup Page Submission] Company Name - Your Name
    Attach
    The completed HTML file and every image used by the page.
    Send the completed page
02

You need a team

Find a team or share your idea

Check the current listings before creating a new one. Contact an existing team directly when there is a match; otherwise, submit your own founder profile.

  1. 1

    Browse current team listings

    Visit Find Teams and check whether an existing idea or team matches your interests. If you find a match, open its profile and contact the proposer directly. If no listing matches, continue to Step 2.

    Browse Find Teams
  2. 2

    Download the Founder Profile template

    Use this template to describe the idea and the collaborators you hope to find.

    Preview templateDownload HTML
  3. 3

    Customize the template

    Replace the visible example content and every team-* metadata value with your information. Set team-directory to profile, introduce the idea, yourself, the current progress, and the roles or skills you are looking for. Delete the template's download area before submission.

  4. 4

    Send the completed listing

    Email the completed HTML file to sl64343@uga.edu and copy tliu@uga.edu. Briefly introduce yourself and your idea in the email.

    Subject
    [Team Listing] Project Name - Your Name
    Attach
    The completed Founder Profile HTML file.
    Send the team listing
